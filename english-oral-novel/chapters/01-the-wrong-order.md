# Chapter 01: The Wrong Order

id: 01
title: The Wrong Order
minutes: 6
scene: South Gate Coffee, Chengdu, Monday 8:47 a.m.
functions: ordering coffee, apologizing, small talk, walking together, making a simple plan

## Story

[narration] Monday. Lin is late. The coffee shop is packed.
Lin: Hi. One oat latte to go, please.
Barista: Name for the order?
Lin: Lin. L-I-N.
Barista: Got it.
[narration] She checks her phone. Standup is in ten minutes.
Lin: Oh no.
Alex: Long line, right?
Lin: Yeah. I usually come earlier.
Alex: Same. First week here. I keep getting the time wrong.
Barista: Oat latte for Lin?
Lin: Thanks.
Alex: Wait. Sorry. I think that's mine.
Lin: This one?
Alex: Yeah. I ordered an oat latte too. Name's Alex.
Lin: Oh. My bad. Here you go.
Alex: No worries. Easy mix-up.
Barista: And an oat latte for Alex. Two of them.
Lin: Then this one is mine.
Alex: Perfect.
Lin: Are you heading to the office too?
Alex: South Gate? Third floor? Yeah. I start today.
Lin: You're the new designer. I'm Lin. Product.
Alex: Nice to meet you, Lin.
Lin: Nice to meet you too. We can walk together, if you want.
Alex: That'd be great. I still get lost.
[narration] They step outside. A scooter goes by.
Lin: How do you like Chengdu so far?
Alex: I like it. It's warm. And the food is a lot.
Lin: In a good way?
Alex: In a very good way. I just don't know what to order.
Lin: I can show you a place at lunch. If you're free.
Alex: I'm free. I'd like that.
Lin: Cool. We should move. Standup is at nine.
Alex: After you.
Lin: If you need anything today, just ask.
Alex: I will. Thanks for not stealing my coffee.
Lin: Hey. I only borrowed it.

## Say These

- One oat latte to go, please. | 外带燕麦拿铁，麻烦了。
- I think that's mine. | 这个应该是我的。
- My bad. Here you go. | 我的错，给你。
- No worries. Easy mix-up. | 没事，很容易拿错。
- Are you heading to the office too? | 你也去公司吗？
- We can walk together, if you want. | 要是你愿意，我们一起走。
- That'd be great. I still get lost. | 太好了，我还是会迷路。
- I can show you a place at lunch. | 中午我可以带你去一个地方。
- If you're free. | 你要是有空的话。
- If you need anything today, just ask. | 今天需要什么，直接问我就行。

## Role Play

You are Lin. You just picked up the wrong coffee.

1. Say it is not yours and give it back.
2. Check if they are going to the same office.
3. Offer to walk together.
4. Invite them to lunch, but keep it light.

Then switch. You are Alex, first week in town.

1. Apologize for the mix-up.
2. Say you still get lost.
3. Accept the lunch invite.

## Notes

- to go | 外带。for here 才是堂食。
- My bad. | 熟人/轻松场合认错。正式场合用 Sorry about that.
- I think that's mine. | 比 That's mine 客气，适合对陌生人。
- if you want / if you're free | 把邀请变软，对方好拒绝。
- That'd be great. | 答应邀请的短口块，比 Yes, I would 自然。
- just ask | 收尾用，表示你可以来找我。
