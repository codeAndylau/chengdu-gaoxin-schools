# Chapter 02: What's Good Here

id: 02
title: What's Good Here
minutes: 6
scene: A small lunch place near the office, Monday 12:10 p.m.
functions: asking for help, talking spice, ordering food, sharing, paying

## Story

[narration] Lunch. They sit down at a small place nearby.
Waiter: Two?
Lin: Yeah. This table is fine.
[narration] The waiter puts down two menus.
Alex: I can't read this. Can you help me?
Lin: Sure. You like spicy food?
Alex: A little. Not too spicy.
Lin: Okay. No super spicy. Got it.
Waiter: Ready to order?
Lin: Almost. What's good here?
Waiter: Mapo tofu. And the garlic pork.
Alex: Is the tofu very spicy?
Waiter: Medium. We can make it mild.
Alex: Mild, please. I still want to taste it.
Lin: Let's get that. And the garlic pork.
Alex: Can we share?
Lin: Yeah. Sharing is easier.
Waiter: Rice?
Lin: Two bowls, please.
Waiter: Anything to drink?
Alex: Just water for me.
Lin: Same. Thanks.
[narration] The food comes fast.
Alex: This smells so good.
Lin: Try the tofu first. It's not too hot.
Alex: Oh. It's warm, but I like it.
Lin: See? You can eat this.
Alex: What's this green stuff?
Lin: Cilantro. Move it if you want.
Alex: I'm okay. How do I say it's too spicy?
Lin: Just say "too spicy, please." That's enough.
Alex: Got it. This pork is really good.
Lin: I know, right?
Alex: I might come back tomorrow.
Lin: We can. It's close.
Waiter: All good?
Lin: Yes. Can we get the bill?
Waiter: Together or separate?
Alex: I can get it. You showed me the place.
Lin: Next time is on me.
Alex: Deal.

## Say These

- I can't read this. Can you help me? | 这个我看不懂，能帮我吗？
- A little. Not too spicy. | 一点点辣就行，不要太辣。
- What's good here? | 这儿有什么好吃的？
- Is it very spicy? | 这个很辣吗？
- Can we make it mild? | 可以做成微辣吗？
- Can we share? | 可以一起分着吃吗？
- Just water for me. | 我喝水就行。
- This is really good. | 这个真的好吃。
- Can we get the bill? | 买单。
- Together or separate? | 一起付还是分开付？
- Next time is on me. | 下次我请。

## Role Play

You are Alex. You cannot read the menu.

1. Ask for help.
2. Say you only want a little spice.
3. Ask if a dish is too spicy.
4. Ask to share.

Then switch. You are Lin.

1. Ask what's good here.
2. Order two dishes and rice.
3. Ask for the bill.
4. Say next time is on you.

## Notes

- What's good here? | 请人推荐，比 What's your favorite 更常用。
- mild | 微辣。not too spicy 是先把底线说清。
- Can we share? | 两人点菜时很实用，避免各点一份太多。
- Just water for me. | 拒饮料的短句。Same 可以接在后面。
- Can we get the bill? | 比 Check, please 更口语。Check 也听得懂。
- Together or separate? | 服务员常用。不会说就回 Together, please.
- Next time is on me. | 这次对方请，你记下一次。on me = 我请客。
